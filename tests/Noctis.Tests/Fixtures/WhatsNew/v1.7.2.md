## Download

Pick your platform from the **Assets** below:

| Platform | Files |
|----------|-------|
| **Windows** | `Noctis-v1.7.2-Setup.exe` (installer) · `Noctis-windows-x64.zip` (portable) |
| **macOS** | `Noctis-osx-arm64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) · `Noctis-osx-x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| **Linux** | `Noctis-x86_64.AppImage` · `Noctis-linux-amd64.deb` · `Noctis-linux-x64.tar.gz` · `Noctis-linux-arm64.tar.gz` |
| **Self-hosted server** | `noctis-server-linux-x64.tar.gz` · `noctis-server-linux-arm64.tar.gz` (see [docs/SELF-HOSTING.md](https://github.com/heartached/Noctis/blob/main/docs/SELF-HOSTING.md)) |

> **Running on macOS** — the app isn't code-signed, so Gatekeeper quarantines it on first launch. After dragging **Noctis** into your Applications folder, clear the flag once in Terminal:
> ```
> xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Noctis.app
> ```
> Then open Noctis normally.
