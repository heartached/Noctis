Lyrics that grow letter by letter as they are sung.

## What's Changed

### Improved

- Improved letters rising one after another as the highlight reaches them, then holding until the line ends.
- Improved timing so the last letters of a word finish on their note.
- Improved long notes growing and glowing like the native version.

### Fixed

- Fixed sung words settling or shaking mid-line.
