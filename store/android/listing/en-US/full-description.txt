Noctis plays the music you already own, from folders on your phone, and shows its lyrics the way they were written: word by word, in time with the song.

WORD-SYNCED LYRICS
• Apple Music-style TTML with word-level timing, with the translation and romanization lines shown under the original.
• Enhanced (word-synced) LRC and standard LRC: the current line lights up and the page follows the song.
• Found automatically: put a .ttml, .elrc or .lrc file with the same name next to the song, or keep lyrics embedded in the file's tags.
• Turn translation and romanization on or off on the lyrics page. Noctis remembers your choice.
• Lyrics follow Android's font size setting.

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR FILES
• Choose the folders that hold your music. Noctis reads only those folders and asks for no storage or media permission.
• Your library is built from your files' own tags and embedded cover art.
• Plays MP3, FLAC, AAC/M4A, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV.
• Album pages with the full track list, and artist pages with their albums.
• Search your whole library by song, album or artist.
• Dark, light and themed looks, with your own accent colour.

PLAYBACK
• Keeps playing in the background, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
• Gapless playback, shuffle, repeat and a play queue.
• Picks up where you left off after the app is closed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Nothing about you or your music leaves your phone.

OPEN SOURCE
Noctis is free and open source (MIT license) and also available for Windows, macOS and Linux. Source code, bug reports and feature requests: https://github.com/heartached/Noctis

Noctis does not include or stream any music. It plays audio files you already have on your device.
